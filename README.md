# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: *Grown Ups*

## 📖 Overview
*Grown Ups* is a **2010 comedy film** directed by **Dennis Dugan** and written by **Adam Sandler** and **Fred Wolf**.  
The movie follows a group of childhood friends who reunite after 30 years to celebrate the life of their late basketball coach.

---

## 🌟 Main Cast
- **Adam Sandler** as Lenny Feder 🏀  
- **Kevin James** as Eric Lamonsoff 😂  
- **Chris Rock** as Kurt McKenzie 🎤  
- **David Spade** as Marcus Higgins 😎  
- **Rob Schneider** as Rob Hilliard 🧘  

---

## 💡 Why I Love It
> *Grown Ups* is lighthearted, hilarious, and full of friendship.  
> It reminds me how important it is to enjoy life and laugh with people who matter.
